		Quality Rules

	Approve

	1.Clear audio
	2.Correct transcript
	3.Correct language
	4.Duration greater than 1 second

	Review
	1. Minor background noise
	2. Unclear pronunciation
	3. Possible transcript mismatch

	Reject
	1.Empty transcript
	2.Corrupted
	3.Wrong language
	4.severe noise
	5.Duration less than 1 second
