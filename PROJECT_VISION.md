SpeechFlow AI

Vision

SpeechFlow AI is a platform for collecting,Validating, and managing Indian-language speech datasets for AI training.

The goal is to simplify speech data collection while ensuring data quality through automation and AI-assisted validation.

Problem

High-quality speech datasets for Indian languages such as Marathi,Gujarati, and Hindi are limited.

Collecting and validating data is often manaual, slow and inconsistent.

Solution

SpeechFlow AI provides:

   Audio upload portal
   Transcript submission
   AI validation agents
   Dataset export system

USERS

Contributor

Uploads speech recordings

Reviewer

Validates recordings and transcripts.

Admin

Manages platform settings and users.
